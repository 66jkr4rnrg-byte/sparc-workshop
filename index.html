About the workshop

Where fundamental research meets emerging technology.

SPARC is designed to encourage knowledge exchange across the rapidly evolving fields of metasurfaces, metamaterials, and terahertz photonics.

The workshop will provide a forum to discuss new concepts, experimental and computational methods, and potential applications of engineered photonic structures. Participants will be able to learn from experts, explore current research directions, and connect with peers across academic and industrial communities.

*Specific hands-on sessions, laboratory visits, and software training should be listed only after confirmation by the organizing committee.

Technical themes

Topics at the frontier of photonics.

The following topics are suggested from the workshop theme. The final scope can be refined by the organizers.

01 / MATERIALS

Metamaterials & Metasurfaces

Engineered electromagnetic responses, resonances, and wavefront control.

02 / SPECTRUM

Mid-IR & Terahertz Photonics

Generation, manipulation, detection, and applications of THz waves.

03 / DEVICES

Flat Optics & Meta-devices

Compact optical components, beam shaping, lenses, and functional surfaces.

04 / PHYSICS

Plasmonics & Light–Matter Interaction

Enhanced field confinement and light interaction with structured materials.

05 / SYSTEMS

Integrated & Silicon Photonics

Photonic platforms, guided-wave devices, and integration strategies.

06 / FRONTIERS

Active, Tunable & Chiral Structures

Reconfigurable responses, polarization control, and emerging applications.
